# NYSTHI port map — batch 3

23 more of the small modules. Running total: 61 modules, 572 ports.

Highlights, where the panel said something a name never could:

- **Hot Tuna** — the signal in and its through are audio; the tuning reference, the octave in/out pair and the frequency-to-voltage output are all volt per octave.
- **DX7 Envelope** — two VCAs (audio in and out), a gate in and its through, and both the envelope and its inverse as CV.
- **Expired Time** — a stopwatch: start and reset in, their throughs, and three alarm outputs, every one a gate.
- **Master Recorder 2** — in and monitor are audio, but start and stop and their throughs are gates.
- **Quad Simpler Slicer Quantizer** — four CV pairs, despite "Simpler" in the name suggesting a sampler.
