## Clarity — Clarity

Maker's own summary: User interface clarity: a patch you can read at a glance. Every port tells you what it is without reading the panel — colour for the kind of signal, and a ring that says input or output, the same on every module, whoever wrote it. Cables take the colour of where they go, with dashes crawling along them, so there is never a moment wondering which way a signal is flowing. Trace one cable through a tangle, pinch to zoom, and pick cables up with a click. Each of these is a switch on the panel, so what it does is what you can see.
