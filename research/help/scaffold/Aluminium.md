## Zones — Zones

Maker's own summary: Splits a polyphonic MIDI-CV keyboard signal (V/OCT, GATE, VELOCITY, AFTERTOUCH, RETRIGGER) into two note-range zones, each independently configurable as polyphonic passthrough or monophonic with note-priority (last/highest/lowest) and true legato gate behavior

## Pads — Pads

Maker's own summary: 16 gate outputs, one per learned note, driven by a polyphonic V/OCT + GATE cable (e.g. from Core MIDI-CV or Zones) instead of MIDI — learn a cell by clicking it and playing (or typing) the note; each cell can be switched to Latch mode to toggle on/off instead of following the held note

Tags: Utility, Polyphonic
