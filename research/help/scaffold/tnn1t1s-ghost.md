## GhostCtrl — GHOST CTRL

Maker's own summary: Global state controller for the Ghost drum system. DEFAULT, ACCENT A, ACCENT B, and MASTER controls are broadcast to adjacent Ghost voices via the expander path. Hit-time events such as trigger and local/total accent gates are patched directly to each voice. Use GHOST CTRL to make the separate voices behave like one instrument.

Tags: Controller, Utility, Expander

## GhostMix — GHOST MIX

Maker's own summary: Dedicated summing mixer for the Ghost drum kit. Twelve labeled inputs -- one per voice of the full kit (Kick, Snare, Rim, Clap, Tom Lo/Mid/Hi, Closed Hat, Open Hat, Crash, Ride) plus a MIXIN channel (the 909's external mix-in) -- each with a mute switch, summed to one MIX output. Per-voice level lives on the voices, so the mixer is a clean unity summer -- the whole kit on one master mix point, in the box.
