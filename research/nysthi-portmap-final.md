# NYSTHI port map — final for this session

NYSTHI 2.4.23. **121 of 149 modules, 1,632 ports** written as Clarity port overrides: 694 control voltage, 534 gate or trigger, 348 audio, 56 volt per octave.

Every entry was read from the module's own panel with each port index drawn onto its jack, cross-checked against parameter names, panel text, the community manual, and in a few cases port names the maker did set.

## What is not in it, and why

**24 modules untouched.** Two kinds:

- **No panel I could match** — YYdisplay, BZ-MAPPER, BZ-XPAND, SimplerTapeControl. Their artwork is not named after the model and I could not identify it.
- **Too complex to read honestly** — Musical Box and Musical Box 2, the mix4/8/16 mixers, AttackSustainRelease 8 and 16, Squonk, Jooper, RAEL, The Cage, Phasor, 4Hands, the Seven Seas family, Bivio, confusingSimpler, QuadSimpler in part. These are dense grids of forty to two hundred ports where a wrong guess would be wrong many times over.

- **Meters and graphers** — PolyVoltageMeter, the Pleasures Grapher. They take anything; off-white is correct.

**Partly done**: 8AttackDecay and AttackSustainRelease4 have their trigger inputs and envelope outputs only; the inner rows are unlabelled and I left them.

## Where the panels earned their keep

- **Bitshifter** is the one NYSTHI module that names its ports properly — "Clock ramp up", "Column 7 Voltage" — and reading them took no picture at all.
- **Sussudio** names 320 parameters and not one port, but the parameter names line up column by column with the jacks: Start Trigger, Stop Trigger, Speed CV, Octave, Volume. Its 100-odd ports came from that alone.
- **Hot Tuna**, **Pitch2Voltage**, **Microtonal Host Helper** and **Tunathor** each mix audio, CV and volt-per-octave on one small panel — exactly the distinctions no name-based rule could ever make.

## If you want the rest

The 24 left are a day's work rather than an hour's, and most of them would be better served by you patching one and telling me what it does than by me squinting at a grid. Clarity's right-click override writes to the same file, so anything you correct by hand simply wins.
